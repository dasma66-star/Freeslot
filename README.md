# FreeSlot

**A collaborative student timetable and meeting scheduling system**

FreeSlot is a web application that helps university students find a time to meet when everyone in a group is available. Students can enter their weekly timetables, create or join groups, and review shared free-time slots before confirming a meeting.

## The problem

Students often have difficulty finding a time that works for group assignments, project discussions, and study sessions. Comparing lecture, laboratory, and tutorial schedules through messages or screenshots can be slow and confusing, leading to delays or missed meetings.

FreeSlot brings timetable and availability information together to help groups find and confirm a suitable meeting time.

## Features

- **Timetable entry:** Add activities with a day, start time, and end time.
- **Group creation and membership:** Create a group and invite members to share their timetables.
- **Automatic free-time detection:** Find intervals when group members are available at the same time.
- **Meeting preferences:** Specify a meeting duration and, where supported, preferred days or times.
- **Meeting selection and confirmation:** Review suggested slots and confirm a meeting time with the group.
- **Personal dashboard:** View your timetable, available periods, and upcoming group meetings.

## Intended users

FreeSlot is designed for university and tertiary-level students coordinating group assignments, capstone projects, and study sessions.

## Working agreements

- Each team member commits to GitHub under their own name and writes clean, commented code.
- Changes go through a pull request and are reviewed by another team member; nobody pushes directly to `main`.
- Each person posts a short progress update to the group before the weekly meeting.
- The team agrees on a weekly meeting time at its first meeting and keeps that time.
- Raise blockers in the group chat on the day they occur.
- Agree role changes with Daniel so the team roster stays current.
- Designers hand finished wireframes to the front-end team before screen development.
- The front-end and back-end teams agree on shared data formats early.
